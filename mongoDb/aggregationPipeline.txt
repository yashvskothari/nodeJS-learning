aggregation pipelines in mongoDB are used to analyze and tranfrom data.They work in stages , each stage performs an operation, and the output of one stage becomes the input of the next.

db.<collection_name>.aggregate([
  { / * stage 1 * /}
  { / * stage 2 * /}
  { / * stage 3 * /}
  .
  .
  .
  .
]);

mongoDB supports over 25 stages of aggregation


$match - works like "find"

$project : display particular fields

$group : group and calculate totals

$match , $project , $group, $add, $sort, $multiply...... are known as accumulators
